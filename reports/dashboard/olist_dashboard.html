July 2018: GMV BRL 1.04M, up 79.7% on July 2017; late deliveries rose to 3.4% from 1.2% in Jun 2018

Jun 2018 had the lowest late share of any month in the window (1.2%), so the rise looks bigger than it is: Jul 2018 is still below both the 12-month pooled late share (Aug 2017 – Jul 2018: 7.6%, all late orders over all delivered orders) and the median month (5.1%).

Portfolio project: the requirements are simulated (no real client), the data is the public Olist dataset. Cards show July 2018, the last complete month; definitions are in docs/dashboard_spec.md.
